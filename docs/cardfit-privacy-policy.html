Privacy Policy for CardFit

Last updated: 13 June 2026

CardFit is developed and published by Bayaan Consultancy. The short version: CardFit does all of its work on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

1. No data collection

We do not collect, store on any server, or transmit your personal data. The app has no user accounts and no login, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. The app does not request internet access permission.

2. What the app processes, and where

CardFit lets you scan identity cards and capture or select photos, lay them out, and export them as PDF or JPEG files. All of this — image capture, cropping, text recognition, optional background removal, layout, and file generation — happens entirely on your device. Your documents and photos never leave your device through the app.

3. Camera and photos

The camera permission is used only to capture documents and photos at your request. Images you capture or select are processed on your device and saved only where you choose (such as your device's Downloads or Documents). The app does not upload them anywhere.

4. On-device intelligence

CardFit uses Google's on-device machine-learning components for document scanning, text recognition, and optional background removal. These run locally on your device. The underlying models are provided and updated by Google Play services as part of your device's platform; the app itself does not send your images or text to any server.

5. Files and sharing

Exported files are stored on your device. If you choose to share or upload a file, that happens through whatever other app you select (for example your email app or a web browser). From that point, the receiving app's and service's own policies apply, not ours.

6. Task data

If you use Task mode, the documents, photos, and names you enter are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted. Deleting a task, or uninstalling the app, removes this data from your device.

7. Children

CardFit is a utility for handling official documents and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

8. Your control and data retention

Because all data stays on your device, you remain in full control of it. Uninstalling the app removes its data. We hold no copy of anything you process.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
