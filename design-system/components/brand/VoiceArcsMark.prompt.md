The canonical Bayaan brand mark — concentric "voice arcs" radiating from a dot; use it anywhere a logo mark is needed (headers, icon tiles, loaders).

```jsx
<VoiceArcsMark size={36} />
<VoiceArcsMark size={36} light />  {/* on midnight surfaces */}
```

Never redraw it; the arc geometry and colors (#14B8A6 / #2A2F6B / #5DCAA5, dot #1E2150) are fixed.
